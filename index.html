Framework Vault

Time-tested mental models for decision-making, purpose, and growth.

🔲 Eisenhower Matrix

Sort tasks by urgency and importance into four quadrants: Do (urgent + important), Schedule (important, not urgent), Delegate (urgent, not important), and Delete (neither).

The insight: most people spend their lives in "urgent" mode and neglect what truly matters. Flip the ratio.

🎯 Ikigai

Your "reason for being" lives at the intersection of four circles: what you love, what you're good at, what the world needs, and what you can be paid for.

You don't need all four to start. Even two overlapping circles create meaning. Begin where you are.

🌱 Kaizen

Continuous improvement through small, consistent steps. The Japanese philosophy that revolutionized manufacturing — and applies to personal growth.

1% better every day compounds to 37x improvement in a year. The key is showing up, not being perfect.

📋 SMART Goals

Make goals Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. Vague wishes become actionable plans.

"Get fit" becomes "Run 5km in under 30 minutes by December 1." Precision creates momentum.

🤖 What the Swarm Does

The 300 agents are a living meditation. Each dot is an independent decision-maker that flock together like birds, fish, or neurons. They respond to you.

QUICK START
  • 👆Move your cursor — agents swarm toward you
  • ✊Click & hold — they scatter like startled fish
  • 👆👆Double-click — respawn all 300 randomly
  • 🖱️Leave the canvas — they wander freely
3 WAYS TO USE IT
🧘 Focus Mode
Breathe slowly. Move your cursor in a circle. Watch the swarm follow. When your mind wanders, the swarm wanders. Bring it back. Repeat for 2 minutes.
🎯 Decision Helper
Think of a yes/no question. Move left for "no," right for "yes." Whichever side the majority of agents settle on first — trust that.
😤 Stress Release
Click and drag wildly. Scatter them. Then stop. Watch them regroup and reconnect. So will you.
AGENT TYPES
  • ● Scouts — fast explorers
  • ● Guards — steady protectors
  • ● Builders — slow constructors
  • ● Gatherers — balanced scouts
  • ● Relays — speedy messengers
